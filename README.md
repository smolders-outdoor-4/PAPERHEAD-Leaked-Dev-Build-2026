## 🕹️ About

PAPERHEAD is a fast-paced first-person shooter with horror and adventure elements, set inside a strange world made of cardboard and paper. Armed with a magical pencil, a shotgun, and brutal movement-based combat, you explore a secret research facility where something has gone horribly wrong. This raw Windows pre-release build surfaced ahead of schedule, unfinished and unpolished.

For players who want a violent, weird, atmospheric first-person shooter with retro movement, horror, gore, and a deliberately unpolished underground feel.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🔫 **Fast-Paced Combat** — Blast, kick, stomp, and launch enemies through frantic boomer-shooter encounters.
- ✏️ **Unusual Arsenal** — Fight with a magical pencil, shotgun, and other weapons.
- 🩸 **Cardboard Horror** — Uncover why a cute paper world has started bleeding.
- 🏭 **Secret Facility** — Explore dark corridors, research spaces, runes, and bloodstained rooms.
- 👁️ **Narrative Horror** — Piece together the mystery through atmospheric moments and environmental clues.
- 💥 **Movement Mayhem** — Use speed, positioning, kicks, explosives, and vertical combat to survive.
- 🎵 **Distinctive Soundtrack** — A tense, aggressive soundtrack drives the strange cardboard nightmare.
